How it works

Pick a subject, learn the idea, then prove it — the hub keeps score so you always know what to study next.

Learn the concept

Each lesson explains the idea in plain language with worked examples and diagrams — no jargon walls.

Practice & check

Answer questions, get step-by-step solutions when you miss, and write in the open-response practice.

Track mastery

A simple grade shows what’s mastered and what’s weak, and spaced review brings topics back right before you’d forget them.

For parents & teachers

You don’t need an account or a dashboard login. A few built-in tools make it easy to point a child or a class in the right direction:

  • Browse by grade — jump straight to what fits a learner’s level.
  • Weekly plans & printables — a ready-to-go Mon–Fri plan and printable practice.
  • Assignments — share a link that opens a specific set of practice; see who finished.
  • A class view — a simple heat-map of strengths and gaps across subjects.
