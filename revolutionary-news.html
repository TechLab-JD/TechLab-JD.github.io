How this works — sources & method

No browser tracking, no server watching you. A scheduled job fetches public headline feeds from the outlets below, groups articles describing the same event by their shared keywords, and writes a static file this page reads. Nothing you click is logged.

Bias ratings describe the outlet, not the individual story, and follow the broad consensus of independent media-bias research (AllSides, Ad Fontes). Reasonable people disagree on the exact placement — that's the point: see the label, then judge the reporting yourself.

Fact-checks come from independent organizations and are matched to stories by topic. A fact-check appearing on a story is not a verdict on that outlet's article — it's related material worth reading.
