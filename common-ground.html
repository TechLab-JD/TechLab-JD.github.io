About this tool — sources & principles

The goal is understanding, not persuasion. Every issue brief is written to pass the other side's test: each camp's view is stated the way a thoughtful person who holds it would recognize — a steelman, never a strawman. If any summary feels unfair to your side, that's a bug worth reporting.

Perception Gap data: More in Common, “The Perception Gap” (June 2019), a national study of how accurately partisans estimate each other's views. “Reality” is the real share of that group holding the view; the comparison bar is what the other party guessed.

Facts vs. values: the facts listed are drawn from non-partisan sources (Pew, Gallup, CDC, Census, CBO, EIA, IPCC and similar). Values questions are left open on purpose — data can inform them, but not decide them. Nothing on this page is logged or tracked.
